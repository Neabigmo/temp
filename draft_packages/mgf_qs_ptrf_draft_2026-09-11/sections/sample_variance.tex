\section{Sample-variance stability}\label{sec:sample-var}

Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be i.i.d. copies of $X$ and
\[
Q_n=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2.
\]
Under the radial-transform discrepancy used in the project, \cref{eq:pointwise-D} gives
\[
\Delta_{\rho'}(X)\le C\sqrt\varepsilon.
\]
Applying \cref{thm:sharp-upper} therefore gives
\[
\dK(X,\Normal(0,1))
\le
C\sqrt{\frac{\log\log(1/\varepsilon)}{\log(1/\varepsilon)}}
\]
for sufficiently small $\varepsilon$; the factors $1/2$ and fixed multiplicative constants inside the logarithms only change the implicit constant.
