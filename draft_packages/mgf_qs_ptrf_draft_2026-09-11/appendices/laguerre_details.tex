\section{Laguerre estimates and construction details}\label{app:laguerre}

For reference, Imekraz--Robert--Thomann normalize the Laguerre function by
\[
\mathcal L_m^{(\alpha)}(r)
 =a_{m,\alpha}L_m^{(\alpha)}(r)e^{-r/2}r^{\alpha/2},
\qquad
a_{m,\alpha}=\sqrt{\frac{m!}{\Gamma(m+\alpha+1)}},
\qquad
\nu=4m+2\alpha+2.
\]
For $\alpha=1/2$, Proposition~3.2 of \cite{ImekrazRobertThomann2016} gives the four estimates
\[
|\mathcal L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|\le C(r\nu)^{1/4}\quad (0\le r\le\nu^{-1}),
\]
\[
|\mathcal L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|\le C(r\nu)^{-1/4}\quad (\nu^{-1}\le r\le\nu/2),
\]
\[
|\mathcal L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|
 \le C\nu^{-1/4}\bigl(\nu^{1/3}+|\nu-r|\bigr)^{-1/4}
 \quad (\nu/2\le r\le3\nu/2),
\]
and an exponential estimate for $r\ge3\nu/2$. Since
\[
\sqrt r\,e^{-r/2}|L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|
 =\sqrt{\frac{\Gamma(m+3/2)}{m!}}\,r^{1/4}|\mathcal L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|
\]
and the gamma ratio is comparable to $\nu^{1/4}$, the first two regions give a uniform bound, the turning region gives $O(\nu^{1/6})$, and the outer region is exponentially decaying. Multiplication by $e^{-r/6}$ therefore yields
\[
\sup_{m\ge1,\,r\ge0}\sqrt r\,e^{-2r/3}|L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|<\infty.
\]

The remaining identities used in Section~\ref{sec:sharpness} are elementary consequences of the Laguerre generating function and orthogonality. In particular, the perturbation is odd, the first $m$ odd moments vanish, and the exact MGF is the expression in \eqref{eq:laguerre-mgf}. The Skellam compensation is chosen after the MGF calculation so that the positive fourth-order hyperbolic remainder dominates the negative term $\log(1-u_m(q)^2)$ pointwise. This order of construction is what makes the one-sided dominance check independent of an asymptotic sign guess.
