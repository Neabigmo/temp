\section{Laguerre input and final sharpness audit}\label{app:laguerre}

For reference, Imekraz--Robert--Thomann normalize the Laguerre function by
\[
\mathcal L_m^\alpha(r)
=a_{m,\alpha}L_m^{(\alpha)}(r)e^{-r/2}r^{\alpha/2},
\qquad
a_{m,\alpha}=\sqrt{\frac{m!}{\Gamma(m+\alpha+1)}},
\]
with
\[
\nu=4m+2\alpha+2.
\]
For $\alpha=1/2$, $\nu=4m+3$ and
\[
Q_m(r)=\sqrt r\,e^{-r/2}|L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|
=a_m^{-1}r^{1/4}|\mathcal L_m^{1/2}(r)|.
\]
Their four-region Proposition~3.2 yields: a uniform bound in the first two regions, $O(\nu^{1/6})$ in the turning region, and exponential decay outside. Multiplication by $e^{-r/6}$ therefore gives
\[
\sup_{m,r}\sqrt r\,e^{-2r/3}|L_m^{(1/2)}(r)|<\infty.
\]

The final pre-submission audit must then verify the remaining internal construction chain:
\begin{enumerate}
\item definition and nonnegativity of the perturbed density/law;
\item exact centering and variance normalization;
\item a uniform exponential-integrability constant;
\item local symmetrized-MGF dominance;
\item defect asymptotics $\log(1/\Delta_m)=m\log m+O(m)$;
\item Kolmogorov separation $\dK(X_m,\Normal(0,1))\ge c m^{-1/2}$.
\end{enumerate}
No novelty or sharpness claim should be submitted before these six items have been independently rechecked.
