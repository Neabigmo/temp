\section{Parameter selection for the sharp upper bound}\label{app:parameters}

This appendix records a non-circular order of choices for the five-step proof. Fix $(\rho,\tau,K)$ and an interior analytic radius. Let $\omega_0>0$ denote the lower bound for the harmonic measure of the real diameter in the smaller half-disk used in the propagation step, and let $C_G$ be the corresponding complex-growth constant.

Choose first $A>8$, and then choose $c_0>0$ so small that
\[
(A+3)c_0<\omega_0.
\]
With
\[
m=\left\lfloor c_0\frac{L}{\log L}\right\rfloor,
\qquad L=\log(1/\Delta),
\]
this makes the fixed-disk Cauchy estimate dominate all factorial losses in the moment-transfer lemma.

Next fix a finite target exponent $\Gamma>4$. Choose the truncation factor $B$ sufficiently large that the tail estimate in Lemma~\ref{lem:tail-lift} has exponent
\[
\beta_B>4\Gamma.
\]
After $B$ is fixed, choose $a_0>0$ so small that the Taylor remainder in Lemma~\ref{lem:real-window} satisfies
\[
-\log(C_Ta_0^2)>4\Gamma,
\]
and, simultaneously,
\[
4(1-\omega_0)(Ba_0+a_0^2)<\frac{\omega_0\Gamma}{2},
\qquad
a_0^2<\frac{\omega_0\Gamma}{4}.
\]
The moment mismatch is already smaller than $e^{-\Gamma m}$ for all sufficiently small $\Delta$ by the choice of $A$ and $c_0$. Hence the Taylor, moment, and truncation errors in Lemma~\ref{lem:real-window} may all be absorbed into a bound of the form
\[
\sup_{|t|\le2R}
\left|\varphi_N(t)\varphi_N(-t)-e^{-t^2}\right|
\le e^{-\Gamma m},
\qquad R=a_0\sqrt m.
\]
Applying the two-constants estimate in Lemma~\ref{lem:zero-free} then gives, after increasing the lower threshold on $m$ if necessary,
\[
\sup_{|z|\le R}
\left|\varphi_N(z)\varphi_N(-z)-e^{-z^2}\right|
\le e^{-\gamma_2m}
\]
with
\[
\gamma_2\ge\frac{\omega_0\Gamma}{2}>a_0^2.
\]
Finally choose the fixed Esseen fraction $0<\theta<1/4$ so that the cubic remainder in the logarithmic expansion is at most one quarter of the Gaussian quadratic damping on $|t|\le\theta R$.

Thus the choices are made in the order
\[
A\longrightarrow c_0\longrightarrow\Gamma\longrightarrow B\longrightarrow a_0\longrightarrow\theta,
\]
and no later choice affects an earlier inequality. All constants depend only on the fixed model parameters and not on $\Delta$ or $m$.
